PROGRESS

What have we done so far?

Our team selected the CMIP6 CESM2-WACCM climate dataset. We explored historical temperature trends from 1950 to 2014. We also planned to examine future projections under the SSP5-8.5 scenario. Our proposal outlines six static visualizations across five continents. This prototype turns the historical warming ranking into an interactive comparison.

DESIGN CHALLENGE

What will be the most challenging part?

The most challenging part will be connecting multiple visualizations into a clear narrative. Historical model results and future climate projections need to be distinguished carefully. We also want interactions that help visitors compare continents without overwhelming them. Communicating uncertainty in climate projections is another important design challenge. Our goal is a useful and accessible visual story.

WHAT COMES NEXT

From prototype to final story

Next, we plan to connect the historical comparison with time-series charts, projected warming scenarios, and geographic views, using validated data and clear explanations.
