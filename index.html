Our climate explorer

Our project looks at how temperatures have changed across five continents and what might happen in the future. So far, we've built an interactive chart where users can compare regions, hover over lines, and explore different years. For our final project, we'll use CMIP6 climate data to show historical trends and future projections. The biggest challenge will be processing the data and making the results easy to understand. Right now, we're using demo data to test the website, and we'll replace it with real data for the final version.

Dataset & Methodology

Planned dataset: CMIP6 (Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6), CESM2-WACCM climate model. We plan to analyze near-surface air temperature (tas) across North America, South America, Europe, Africa, and Asia.

Time periods: Historical simulations from 1950–2014 and future projections from 2020–2099 under SSP5-8.5. Temperature anomalies will be calculated relative to the 1951–1980 baseline.

Source: NOAA Public CMIP6 Dataset on Google Cloud . Continental aggregation and the final model data have not yet been integrated into this prototype.
