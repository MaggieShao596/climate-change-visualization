Our climate explorer

We've built the first version of our climate explorer to compare warming across five continents. The chart lets users hover over lines, highlight a region, and change the year range. We also added historical and future views to make the trends easier to explore. Right now, we're using clearly labeled demo data while we prepare the real CMIP6 dataset. The hardest part will be processing the large climate files into accurate continent-level data. We'll need to calculate temperature anomalies using the same 1951–1980 baseline for every region. We also want to keep the chart easy to read when the five lines overlap. Another challenge is showing future SSP5-8.5 projections clearly without making them look like certain predictions.

Dataset & Methodology

Planned dataset: CMIP6 (Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6), CESM2-WACCM climate model. Our project focuses on near-surface air temperature (tas) across North America, South America, Europe, Africa, and Asia.

Time periods: Historical 1950–2014 and future 2020–2099 under the SSP5-8.5 scenario. We plan to calculate temperature anomalies relative to the 1951–1980 baseline. Note: the CMIP6 historical experiment ends in 2014 and the SSP experiments begin in 2015, so 2015–2019 is currently not shown in either view.

Data source: NOAA Public CMIP6 Dataset on Google Cloud . The real model data has not yet been integrated into this initial prototype.
