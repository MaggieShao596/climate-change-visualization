PROTOTYPE NOTES

What have we done so far?

Our team selected the CMIP6 CESM2-WACCM climate dataset and defined five continents for comparison. We analyzed historical simulations from 1950 to 2014 and future projections under SSP5-8.5 from 2020 to 2099. We developed six static visualizations for our proposal, covering historical anomalies, continental comparisons, projected warming and latitude. In this prototype, we have translated the proposal's reported summary values into interactive D3 charts, and retained the original figures where full underlying series are not yet available. We will replace the remaining static figures with interactive versions as we prepare the data.

What will be the most challenging part to design, and why?

The main challenge is bringing several different chart types into one coherent visual story. Historical simulations and future projections use different time periods, so readers need clear context to compare them responsibly. We also need to process large climate datasets into lightweight files that can load quickly in a browser. Designing meaningful filters across the charts without losing clarity will require iteration and testing. Finally, we must communicate that our results come from one model and a high-emissions scenario, not guaranteed outcomes.
