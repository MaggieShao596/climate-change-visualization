PROJECT OVERVIEW

Our climate explorer

Our project explores how temperature anomalies vary across five continents and how regional warming may change in the future. We have built an interactive line chart where visitors can compare regions, hover to inspect values, highlight a continent, and adjust the year range. The website also lets users switch between historical and future views to explore different time periods. For the final project, we plan to use the CMIP6 CESM2-WACCM near-surface air temperature dataset, covering 1950–2014 for historical simulations and 2020–2099 for SSP5-8.5 projections. We will calculate continental temperature anomalies relative to the 1951–1980 baseline. Our main challenge is processing large climate model files into accurate regional time series while making overlapping lines and future projections easy to understand. We also need to communicate that climate projections depend on model assumptions and are not certain predictions. For now, the chart uses illustrative placeholder values rather than real CMIP6 results, and the CO₂ view is also a demo; we will replace these before the final submission.

Planned data source: NOAA Public CMIP6 Dataset on Google Cloud.
